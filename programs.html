KHDA-Approved Course

Engaging Skeptical Minds (ESM)

A professional development course for parents, mentors and educators — approved by Dubai’s Knowledge and Human Development Authority (KHDA). Not for children.

Young people today raise serious questions about identity, worldview, meaning and truth, often long before the adults around them feel ready. ESM builds that readiness: how to listen properly, how to reason clearly, and how to stay the trusted voice in a young person’s life.

  • FormatInstructor-led sessions with discussion and case work.
  • AudienceParents, educators, mentors and youth leaders.
  • LocationDubai Outsource City campus — online cohorts on request.
  • IntakesRun periodically through the year. Contact us for the next dates.
