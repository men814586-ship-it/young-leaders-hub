Home / Campus

A campus built for building things

Located on the 5th floor of the Arab Bank Corporate Office in Dubai Outsource City — purpose-arranged labs, studios and collaboration spaces.

Robotics & Engineering Lab

Workbenches, kits, sensors and tools where students design, assemble and test working machines.

AI & Computing Studio

Workstations for coding, model training, data work and digital design.

Science & STEM Space

Equipment for real experiments — measurement, observation and investigation.

Presentation Hall

A proper stage with audio and screen for public speaking, debates and pitch days.

Media & Cinematography Corner

Cameras, lighting and editing stations for student film production.

Fitness & Sports Sessions

Coach-led physical activity built into the weekly timetable.

Quiet Study & Reading Area

Focused space for reading, revision and one-to-one mentoring.

Collaboration Zones

Open group tables where project teams plan, argue and iterate together.

Safe & Supervised

Secure corporate-building access with supervised entry, exit and break times.
