Empowering today’s youth as tomorrow’s leaders

Young Leaders Hub is a Dubai-based learning institute where children learn, evolve, indulge their curiosity and stay ahead in the race.

Our Story

Built for the world children will actually enter

We started Young Leaders Hub because we kept meeting bright, hard-working children who could pass any exam — and freeze the moment they were asked to solve something nobody had solved for them first.

The gap was never intelligence. It was exposure: to real problems, to real tools, to adults who had built things, and to the habit of being responsible for an outcome. So we built a place where academics stay strong and everything missing gets added back.

Today, from our campus in Dubai Outsource City, our mentors work with children in small groups on projects they present and defend — growing them academically, socially, physically and, above all, in character.

Our Mission

To empower today’s youth as tomorrow’s leaders — equipping every child with the academic depth, professional skill and moral character to lead.

Our Vision

A generation that does not just consume the future, but designs it — confident, curious, principled and prepared.

Our Values

Curiosity over compliance. Character before credentials. Understanding above memorisation. Community over competition.

Our Pillars

Five commitments behind every program

These are not marketing lines — they are the criteria we use to accept or reject anything into the curriculum.

01

Hands-On Practical Learning

If a child cannot build it, test it or explain it, we have not taught it yet.

02

Professional Skills with a Leadership Focus

Communication, ownership and teamwork treated as core subjects, not soft extras.

03

Qualification in STEM & AI

Structured, progressive pathways in the technologies that will define their careers.

04

World-Class Instructors

Mentors drawn from engineering, medicine, banking, sport and scholarship.

05

Sports & Wellbeing

Coached sport and physical activity built into the week, because focus is physical too.
